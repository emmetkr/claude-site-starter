# Copy rules

These are the rules behind the copy on kartel.ai. Claude follows them on every page.

1. **Say the thing.** No "X, not Y". No "rather than". No "not just". State what it is.
2. **No tails.** Do not add "so you can…" or "which means…" to a sentence. State the fact and stop.
3. **No fragment headlines.** No "Faster. Smarter." No participle tails ("Built for teams, designed for speed."). A heading is a sentence or a plain noun phrase.
4. **Full sentences in captions.** A caption under an image is a sentence, not a label.
5. **Name the feature.** "Image and video editing", not "Creative magic".
6. **Numbers you can check.** If a number is on the page, it has a source you could show someone.
7. **Reading size.** Nothing under 15px in running copy.
8. **One idea per sentence.** Under 20 words where you can. Active voice.
9. **No marketing adjectives.** Seamless, powerful, cutting-edge, effortless: cut them. Replace with the measurement or delete.
10. **The reader's words.** Write to one reader, in the words they would use to describe their own problem.
